## About Me

I am a Staff Research Scientist at Ant Group. Here, I lead a growing team focused on advancing multimodal foundation models, with an emphasis on post-training and agent technologies.

My journey into AI began at the University of Oxford, where I earned a PhD in Physics and subsequently conducted postdoctoral research at the renowned Visual Geometry Group (VGG). There, my work on AI-driven medical imaging set a new world record in brain age prediction. Following my time in academia, I joined Google as a Machine Learning Engineer, leading computer vision and LLM-agent solutions for large-scale smart manufacturing.

My research interests lie in computer vision, multimodal foundation models, and bridging cutting-edge research with real-world applications.
