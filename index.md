## What's going on? ##
- ***[CNN in spectrum image](/projects/cnn/).*** I am now working on using convolutional neural network to enhance the spectrum image features. Using CNN in scientific data can be tricky. The question is not only how well it works, but also **how** it works. Update coming soon...
- ***[Neuromics project](/projects/neuro/).***  I worked on some beautiful neuronal image data with [Prof Kenneth Harris](http://www.ucl.ac.uk/cortexlab), who led me into the fantastic field of neuroscience. The open question was that could we register a particular ex-vivo microscopic 2D image slice in the in-vivo 3D two-photon volume data? Check out [my answer](/projects/neuro/) at the Neuromics project annual retreat 2017 (and the [GitHub page](https://github.com/ha-ha-ha-han/NeuromicsCellDetection)). [Read more ...](/projects/neuro/)
- ***[Graphene with a twist](http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.201606741/full).*** Stacking 2D materials together is just like playing microscopic Lego. In the bilayer graphene scenario, the twist angle between the two layers is the driving parameter in the twisted bilayer graphene for the photonic device application [\(Yin et al. 2016)](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms10699) and photochemical reactivity [\(Liao et al. 2015)](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acs.nanolett.5b02240). [Peng et al. 2017](http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.201606741/full) explains why by using photoemission spectroscopy to visualize the electronic band structure's evolution with the twist angle. Come to my slot in [APS March meeting 2018](http://meetings.aps.org/Meeting/MAR18/Session/C18.7) and have a talk. [Read more...](/projects/graphene/)
